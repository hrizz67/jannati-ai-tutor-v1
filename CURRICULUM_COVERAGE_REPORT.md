# Jannati AI Tutor V1.5.2 LTS Curriculum Coverage Audit

## Summary
- Audit scope: Bahasa Melayu, Matematik, English, Sains, Pendidikan Islam, Bahasa Arab, Pendidikan Jasmani, Pendidikan Kesihatan.
- Total questions audited: 4000.
- Overall curriculum coverage: 83%.
- Overall quality score: 91%.
- DSKP Curriculum Complete: NO.
- Method note: exact DSKP SK/SP codes are not embedded in most question files, so mappings are conservative and flagged as inferred in CURRICULUM_MATRIX.json.

## Key Finding
The banks are strong for topic-based UASA revision and balanced at 40% mudah, 40% sederhana and 20% sukar across all subjects. However, Jannati AI Tutor should not yet be declared fully DSKP Curriculum Complete because several subjects lack verifiable coverage of oral/listening/practical/activity-based standards and exact SK/SP code metadata.
