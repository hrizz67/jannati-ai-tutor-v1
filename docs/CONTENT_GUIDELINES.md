# Content Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines define how to add and maintain question content for Jannati AI Tutor without changing the learning model.

## How to Add New Questions

1. Add the question to the correct subject file.
2. Ensure the answer is exact and verified.
3. Add accepted answers only when there is a valid equivalent answer.
4. Add a short hint that guides without giving the answer away.
5. Add an explanation that teaches the reason clearly.
6. Set difficulty and curriculum metadata correctly.
7. Validate the entry with the question validator.

## How to Add a New Subject

1. Create the subject data file.
2. Provide subject metadata, topics, and question arrays.
3. Register the subject in the subject index.
4. Verify the subject through curriculum and question validation.

## How to Add a New Topic

1. Choose a clear Year 2 topic name.
2. Write questions that match the skill outcome.
3. Keep hints and explanations topic-specific.
4. Ensure examples match the subject and topic.

## Writing Standards

- Keep language age-appropriate.
- Keep instructions short and clear.
- Use a teacher-like tone.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.

## DBP Guidelines

- Use standard Malaysian Malay spelling and grammar.
- Avoid Indonesian wording.
- Avoid awkward machine-translated phrasing.
- Keep punctuation clean and consistent.

## CEFR Guidelines

- Use Year 2-friendly vocabulary.
- Keep English instructions simple and direct.
- Avoid vocabulary that is too advanced for the target level.

## Math Writing Rules

- Ensure calculations are correct.
- Include units where appropriate.
- Keep word problems concise and solvable.
- Avoid ambiguous distractors.

## Science Writing Rules

- Keep factual statements correct.
- Use simple scientific terminology.
- Make explanations suitable for Year 2.

## Arabic Rules

- Preserve Arabic spelling and rendering.
- Keep RTL handling correct.
- Pair Arabic content with natural Malay support text.

## Islamic Terminology Rules

- Use standard terms such as `Allah SWT`, `Rasulullah SAW`, `solat`, `wuduk`, `doa`, `adab`, and `akhlak`.
- Keep terminology consistent across explanations and hints.

## Hint Rules

- Guide without revealing.
- Keep hints short.
- Make hints relevant to the actual topic.

## Explanation Rules

- Explain the answer clearly.
- Teach the reasoning, not just the label.
- Keep explanations short and useful.

## Tips, Common Mistakes, and Examples

- Tips should be topic-specific.
- Common mistakes should reflect the actual concept.
- Examples should fit the subject, topic, and Year 2 level.
- Avoid copying the same examples across unrelated topics.

## Content Diversity Rules

- Rotate stems where safe and useful.
- Keep meaning stable.
- Avoid overusing the same hints or encouragement phrases.

## Question Quality Checklist

- [ ] Correct answer verified
- [ ] Accepted answers verified
- [ ] Hint is useful and safe
- [ ] Explanation matches the answer
- [ ] Topic relevance is clear
- [ ] Difficulty is appropriate
- [ ] Curriculum mapping is present
- [ ] No placeholder text remains
